Eight jumping efforts over seven numbered obstacles, laid out to ride forward
without ever asking a horse to jump out of a turn it has not had time to
straighten from.

The track opens away from the entrance on a vertical, then turns left onto an
oxer on the long side. Obstacle 3 is a one-stride double, A to B, set at 7.5 m
measured centre to centre — walk it before you build it, because that distance
is the one thing on this course that will not forgive being 40 cm out.

From the double the line runs across the top of the arena to the wall at 4, and
then right-handed down to the Liverpool oxer at 5. The water is the question on
this course. It sits after the longest single straight, so horses arrive with
plenty of pace; if you are building this for a young or green horse, take the
tray out and leave the oxer.

The triple bar at 6 is jumped away from the gate and back towards the middle,
which keeps the last turn honest, and 7 is a plain gate on a slight angle to the
finish. Nothing in the last third is designed to catch anybody out — the course
should be won or lost at 3 and 5.

Measured route length is about 152 m. At 350 m per minute that is a time allowed
of roughly 26 seconds, so set your clock accordingly and remember the app's
figure is the line through the obstacles, not the line a fast horse actually
takes.
