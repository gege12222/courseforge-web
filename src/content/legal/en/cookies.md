## This site sets no cookies

Not necessary ones, not analytics ones, not advertising ones. None. There is no
consent banner because there is nothing to consent to, and a banner that asked
you to agree to nothing would be theatre.

That is unusual enough that it is worth showing the working.

## What the site does store

### Your language choice — `localStorage`, not a cookie

If you switch between English and Hungarian, the site writes your choice to
`localStorage` under a single key so the next page you open is in the language you
picked.

- It is stored on your device and is never sent to a server.
- It holds nothing but the two-letter language code, `en` or `hu`.
- It is a functional preference you actively asked for, which under the EU
  ePrivacy rules does not require consent.
- Clearing your browser's site data removes it. Not switching language means
  nothing is ever written.

That is the only thing this site stores in your browser.

## Analytics without cookies

We use **Cloudflare Web Analytics**, which measures page views without cookies,
without `localStorage`, and without fingerprinting. It cannot follow you to
another site and we cannot single out your visit in it. We see aggregate numbers:
which pages get read, roughly where readers are, which browser family they use.

Because it stores nothing on your device, it needs no consent under the ePrivacy
Directive, and because we cannot identify you from it, it is not a privacy trade
dressed up as a compromise.

## What is deliberately not here

- No Google Analytics, Meta pixel, or any other advertising or profiling tag.
- No embedded YouTube, Vimeo, Google Maps, or social widgets — every one of those
  sets third-party cookies.
- No consent management platform, because there is no consent to manage.
- No fonts, scripts or stylesheets loaded from another company's servers. The
  typeface is served from this domain.

## The app

The CourseForge app is not a browser and uses no cookies. Your courses and
settings are stored in a local database on your device and are not uploaded
anywhere.

The app does use the network in two places, and neither involves cookies: the
free version may show advertising through Google AdMob, and purchases are
verified through the app store and RevenueCat. Both are described in full in the
[privacy policy](/privacy), including how to change your advertising consent.

## If this ever changes

If a feature is ever added that genuinely needs a cookie, this page will say so
before it ships, and if consent is legally required you will be asked for it
properly — with a real choice to decline, and no dark patterns.
